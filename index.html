Data & privacy

Your edits are saved to this browser's localStorage. They do not sync across computers. Browser data clearing may erase them. A failed save restores the last saved version and shows an error; export backups regularly.

—

Back up regularly. Use only school-approved storage and avoid sensitive pupil information on shared devices. This personal planner is not an encrypted school record-management system. Use fictional or school-authorized data only.

First time? Add classes, plan units, create lessons, review your week, then export a backup. Sample and Blank versions save separately.
